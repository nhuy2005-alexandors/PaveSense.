## 1. Project identity and scope

PaveSense is the repository/prototype for the Road Evidence project, previously presented as **“RoadWatch Evidence: AI-Assisted Road Damage Reporting and Evidence Reconciliation”** by **RoadWatch Lab**.

The project analyzes road-surface imagery in Can Tho, Vietnam. It is a course-project prototype that produces reviewable visual evidence; it is **not** an official road-condition certification system and does not determine legal responsibility, repair quality, or government response.

### Intended inference flow

1. Accept a road image or video.
2. Decode video and extract representative frames before inference.
3. Run two complementary image-model branches in parallel on each selected frame:
   - Multi-label image classification: C01–C07.
   - Multi-class object detection: C02–C07.
4. Associate predictions with timestamps and GPS only when valid metadata is available.
5. Aggregate frame evidence into a reviewable route/segment report.

The models consume images/frames, not raw video. Classification and detection are parallel branches, not a serial model chain. Initial application target is a localhost demo.

## 2. Current taxonomy and annotation semantics

The current seven-class taxonomy is defined in `configs/taxonomy.yaml` (version 1, status currently marked `provisional`). Use it as the working annotation baseline while continuing dataset coverage and visual QA.

| ID | Class | Operational definition |
|---|---|---|
| C01 | Normal Road | Road surface appears intact and has no visible target distress in the observable area. A flat, intact, accepted repair patch belongs here if no target defect remains visible. |
| C02 | Longitudinal Crack | Crack whose dominant direction is approximately parallel to the road's longitudinal axis. |
| C03 | Transverse Crack | Crack whose dominant direction is approximately perpendicular to the road's longitudinal axis. |
| C04 | Alligator Cracking | Interconnected fatigue-crack network forming a polygonal/block-like pattern. |
| C05 | Pothole | Localized cavity or hole involving material loss; standing water is an observation condition, not a separate class. |
| C06 | Surface Deterioration | Surface-level breakdown such as raveling, weathering, peeling, or aggregate/material loss without a distinct pothole cavity being the defining feature. |
| C07 | Surface Deformation | Geometric change in the road profile: rutting, depression, upheaval, raised/uneven surface, bumps, or localized settlement/level mismatch. Includes a repaired/reinstated utility or drainage cut when the finished surface remains visibly raised or uneven relative to the surrounding pavement. |

### Binding annotation rules

- **Classify the visible current condition, not the repair history or administrative acceptance status.** There is no separate `repair_status` class or required repair-status dimension in this taxonomy.
- A flush, intact repair patch with no visible distress → **C01**.
- A repaired/reinstated patch that remains raised, depressed, lumpy, or uneven → **C07**. Its being repaired or accepted does not turn the visible geometric deformation into Normal.
- If a patch visibly has a crack, cavity, or surface material loss, label the corresponding C02/C03/C04/C05/C06 condition; multi-label output is allowed when multiple conditions are visible.
- Patching/repaired road sections are repair context, not a standalone damage class.
- C05 is defined by a cavity; C06 covers surface deterioration without a distinct pothole cavity as the defining feature; C07 is about geometry/profile change.
- Determine crack orientation against the road axis, not image edges. Do not infer C02/C03 from a generic source label such as `Linear Crack` without visual orientation evidence.
- Classification is multi-label: one image may contain multiple damage classes. C01 is mutually exclusive with C02–C07.
- Detection contains six foreground classes C02–C07. C01 is a negative/background image and must not receive a Normal bounding box.
- If water, glare, distance, blur, or occlusion prevents reliable judgment, mark the sample uncertain/unassessable rather than guessing.
- Keep one physical image once in the manifest; represent multiple labels/boxes as annotations.

The user-provided raised-repair example discussed on 2026-11-06 establishes the intended semantic boundary for C07. Its location, provenance, and reuse rights are not independently verified here; do not silently treat it as cleared training data.

Use these terms precisely:
- **Published**: reported by the dataset provider.
- **Downloaded**: bytes actually obtained locally.

- **Drive uploaded**: only after the file is present in the target Drive folder and verified by Drive readback.
- **QA accepted**: only after visual/label/provenance review.

Detailed references and embedded images are in the linked **Dataset Preflight & Can Tho Visual Audit** document. Current conclusions:

- **C05 and C06** have the clearest local public visual evidence in the reviewed material.
- **C07** has partial local support, especially rutting/unevenness reported on Tỉnh lộ 920; the available local press evidence is not a substitute for a controlled field set.
- **C02/C03/C04** have public dataset annotations, but the reviewed Can Tho press images are not consistently clear enough to prove subtype separation from a single image.

- A Tuổi Trẻ report (2026-10-07) on Tỉnh lộ 920 through Trà Nóc describes potholes, surface deterioration, roughness, and wheel-path rutting: local context for C05/C06 and some C07.
- VTC News (2025-11-14) on Nguyễn Văn Cừ and other Can Tho roads supports C05/C06 and general crack presence, but not reliable C02/C03/C04 subtype labeling from the reviewed images.
- Lao Động (2025-09-09) under Hưng Lợi bridge documents potholes and standing water: C05 evidence; water is not a new class.
- Báo Cần Thơ (2026-01-01) documents patching at Hưng Phú: repair-context evidence only; it does not automatically mean damage or prove raised reinstatement.
- Tuổi Trẻ (2025-04-23) shows road excavation in progress: evidence of construction activity only, not proof of the post-reinstatement road profile.

Press images embedded in the audit are visual-domain references, not automatically cleared training assets. Verify copyright/license and permissions before copying them into a training set or redistributing them.
